# Abhijay - Portfolio

## Product summary

Public personal portfolio for Abhijay Parija, a CS and Business sophomore at UMass
Amherst. Every featured project has a working deployment, and the site is built so
that fact is the first thing a visitor learns.

## Audience

- Recruiters and engineers evaluating Abhijay's work, usually scanning fast
- Peers looking at hackathon and side projects
- Traffic arriving from LinkedIn and GitHub

## Jobs to be done

1. Understand who Abhijay is within the first viewport
2. Open a live project demo in one click
3. Reach email, GitHub, or LinkedIn without hunting

## The differentiator

Five projects, five live deploys. Most student portfolios link to repositories that
a reader will never clone and run. This one links to software that is already
running. The design leads with that rather than burying it under chrome.

## Brand commitments

- **World:** cinematic dark lobby, amber against deep space, WebGL starfield
- **Voice:** plain and concrete. No filler verbs, no invented precision, no
  performative craftsman labels
- **Proof:** live Render deploys, linked directly from each project
- **Co-credit:** BenchPilot credits Ratnabh and Jingbong; AetherGuard credits Aryan

## Anti-references

- Navy `#0a192f` + mint `#64ffda` developer-portfolio template (what shipped until v3)
- Sumi Village theme (documented in v2 but never actually implemented in CSS)
- Fake operator and control-room HUD labels borrowed from game UI
- Purple SaaS gradients, nested cards, section-number eyebrows

## Surfaces

| Surface | Mode | Goal |
|---------|------|------|
| Home (`/`) | Experience + Persuade | Identity, then the work, then contact |

## Primary CTA

View the work → `#work`

## Contact intent

One label, everywhere: **Get in touch**. It appears in the nav, the hero, and the
closing section, and always resolves to email. Do not introduce a second phrasing.
